Michael Myers FAQ

Can you play as Michael Myers?

Yes. Michael is playable in both the six-chapter single-player story and 1v4 multiplayer.

What are Michael Myers' starting traits?

Killer Sense and Shape Jump are the two traits included in his starting loadout.

How many abilities can Michael equip?

The official progression overview says players select three distinct abilities for Michael's loadout.

What Michael Myers skins have been announced?

Official announcements include Phantom Michael Myers as a pre-order exclusive and Inmate Michael Myers with the Digital Deluxe Edition. The game also supports Killer skins and tints through customization.

Information verified against the official Progression & Customization, Single-Player, and Pre-order announcements.
