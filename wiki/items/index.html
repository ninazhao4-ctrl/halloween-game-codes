Halloween: The Game Items Guide

This guide explains the most important escape and utility items in Halloween: The Game, including Gas Cans, Sedan Keys, Escape Keys, Bolt Cutters, Fuses, and Repair Kits. Item spawns are randomized between matches, so the locations below should be treated as high-value search areas rather than guaranteed fixed spawns.

Quick rule: learn what each item is used for first, then search the most logical nearby buildings instead of relying on one memorized spawn.

Gas Can

The Gas Can is one of the three main items required for the Sedan escape. Civilians also need a Sedan Key and a Repair Kit before the vehicle can be used to leave the map.

Used forSedan escape
Also requiredSedan Key + Repair Kit
Spawn typeRandomized

Best Places to Find Gas

  • Detached and attached garages
  • Backyard sheds and open utility sheds
  • Storage rooms and searchable closets inside houses
  • Commercial back rooms, service alleys, and utility areas
  • Roadside or fence-line areas near major landmarks

Do not treat any single building as a guaranteed Gas spawn. Prioritize garages, sheds, storage spaces, and service areas while rotating toward the Sedan.

Keys

Halloween: The Game uses different keys for different escape routes. The Sedan Key and Escape Key are not interchangeable.

Sedan Key

The Sedan Key is specifically required for the Sedan escape. It does not open Storm Cellar or Escape Gate padlocks.

  • Bedroom nightstands and dressers
  • Hallway and entryway cabinets
  • Living-room storage furniture
  • Kitchen drawers and sideboards
  • Other searchable drawers inside houses

Because spawns are randomized, search every interactive drawer you pass while moving through residential areas rather than relying on one specific house.

Escape Key

The Escape Key, sometimes described as a Padlock Key, is used on locked escape routes rather than vehicles.

  • Drawers inside houses
  • Tables and shelves
  • Floors and room corners
  • Chests and red toolboxes
  • Outdoor porch or deck tables on some maps

The Escape Key can be used on Storm Cellar and Escape Gate padlocks. Bolt Cutters can serve as an alternative.

Bolt Cutters

Bolt Cutters are an alternative to the Escape Key for certain locked escape routes. Their spawn locations are randomized between matches.

Where to Find Bolt Cutters

  • Large drawers
  • Table and shelf surfaces
  • Floors in utility rooms
  • Unlocked chests
  • Red toolboxes

Small drawers and kitchens are lower-priority search areas compared with the locations above.

What Bolt Cutters Open

  • Storm Cellar: can replace an Escape Key on the padlock.
  • Escape Gate: can replace the Exit/Escape Key on the gate lock.

Bolt Cutters do not replace the Fuse required for a powered Escape Gate.

Fuse

The Fuse is used for the powered Escape Gate. It is not required for Sedan or Storm Cellar escapes. Fuse spawns are randomized and can be harder to locate than common loot.

Where to Find a Fuse

  • Lock boxes and locked containers
  • Drawers inside houses
  • Red toolboxes
  • Basements and storage rooms
  • Containers near major landmarks
  • NPC rewards in some situations

Escape Gate Requirements

A Gate escape requires a Fuse plus an Escape Key or Bolt Cutters. If boards block the gate, they may also need to be removed before the route is usable.

Repair Kit

Repair Kits are multi-purpose utility items. Their spawn locations are randomized between matches.

Where to Find Repair Kits

  • Floors, tables, and drawers inside houses
  • Cabinets and storage furniture
  • Garages and attached utility buildings
  • Sheds and storage areas
  • Searchable closets and lockers

What Repair Kits Are Used For

  • Repairing the Sedan for the car escape
  • Restoring damaged Power Boxes
  • Potentially restoring certain damaged utility or communication systems, depending on the current interaction

For the Sedan escape, the Repair Kit is used alongside a Gas Can and Sedan Key.

Best Places to Search First

  1. Garages and sheds: strong first checks for Gas and Repair Kits.
  2. Residential drawers: high-value searches for Sedan Keys and other small items.
  3. Red toolboxes: useful for Bolt Cutters, Escape Keys, and potentially other utility items.
  4. Basements and storage rooms: worth checking for uncommon utility items such as Fuses.
  5. Tables, shelves, and floor loot: do not ignore visible items while moving between rooms.

Halloween: The Game Item FAQ

Where can I find Gas in Halloween: The Game?

Gas spawns are randomized. Garages, sheds, storage areas, searchable closets, commercial service areas, and some outdoor utility spots are good places to search first.

Where can I find a Sedan Key?

Sedan Keys are commonly found in searchable drawers and storage furniture inside houses, including bedrooms, hallways, living rooms, and kitchens.

What do Bolt Cutters open?

Bolt Cutters can replace an Escape Key on Storm Cellar and Escape Gate padlocks.

Where can I find a Fuse?

Fuse spawns are randomized. Useful search areas include locked containers, drawers, red toolboxes, basements, storage rooms, and some NPC rewards.

What is the Repair Kit used for?

Repair Kits are used to repair the Sedan and damaged Power Boxes. They may also support other repairable utility interactions depending on the object and match state.

Are item locations fixed?

No. Item spawns are randomized between matches, so this guide focuses on common search areas and item uses rather than claiming one guaranteed fixed location.
