Character FAQ

How many playable Civilians are in Halloween: The Game?

The Standard Edition includes 10 playable Civilians at launch. Richard and Alexis add two more through the Digital Deluxe Edition.

Can you play as Michael Myers?

Yes. Michael is playable in the single-player story and as the Killer in multiplayer.

Is Laurie Strode playable?

Yes. Laurie Strode is part of the 10-character Standard Edition Civilian roster.

Are character abilities the same for everyone?

No. The official progression overview describes distinct Civilian abilities, starting items, and Killer abilities. Detailed builds will be added as they are verified in-game.
