East Haddonfield Map & Escape Guide

Use the East Haddonfield reference map to orient around building clusters and the map boundary, then plan a storm cellar, escape gate or sedan route using the objectives shown in your current match.

East Haddonfield map

Escape routes

Storm Cellar

Find an active cellar exit, clear its barrier with the item shown by the objective prompt, then complete the interaction. A key or bolt cutters may be needed for a locked or chained entrance.

Escape Gate

Find the active perimeter gate. Check whether it needs a fuse and a key or bolt cutters, then complete the gate interaction.

Sedan

Prepare the car with a repair kit, gas can and sedan keys when required. Once it starts, follow the in-game exit route.

Landmarks

Use the central building cluster, smaller buildings near the map edge and the outlined boundary to orient yourself. Match the icons and objective prompts on your in-game map before choosing an escape route.

FAQ

Do all exits appear in every match?

Do not assume they do. Confirm the active exit and objective requirements in your current match.

What does the sedan need?

Check the objective prompt; common sedan items include a repair kit, gas can and sedan keys.

Can I use bolt cutters at the gate or cellar?

When the active entrance has a chain or lock that accepts them, bolt cutters may help. Follow the prompt at the specific exit.
